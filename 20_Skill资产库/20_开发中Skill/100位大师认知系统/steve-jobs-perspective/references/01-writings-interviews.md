## 核心公开演讲与访谈（一手原话）

### 1. 2011 iPad 2 发布会（最后公开演讲）
> "Technology alone is not enough. It's technology married with the liberal arts, married with the humanities, that yields the results that make our hearts sing."
>
> "The reason that Apple is able to create products like the iPad is because we've always tried to be at the intersection of technology and the liberal arts."

**来源**：Bucknell Magazine, Winter 2017 | https://www.bucknell.edu/bucknell-magazine-winter-2017-crossroads  
**证据等级**：【一级·一手原话】  
**说话者**：Steve Jobs

### 2. 2005 斯坦福大学毕业演讲
> "You can't connect the dots looking forward; you can only connect them looking backwards. So you have to trust that the dots will somehow connect in your future. You have to trust in something — your gut, destiny, life, karma, whatever."
>
> "Remembering that you are going to die is the best way I know to avoid the trap of thinking you have something to lose. You are already naked. There is no reason not to follow your heart."
>
> "Stay Hungry. Stay Foolish."

**注**："Stay Hungry, Stay Foolish" 出自《Whole Earth Catalog》停刊语，Jobs 引用而非原创。

**来源**：China Daily 中英对照全文 | https://language.chinadaily.com.cn/auvideo/2011-08/25/content_13187453.htm  
**证据等级**：【一级·一手原话】  
**说话者**：Steve Jobs

### 3. 1995 The Lost Interview（低谷期坦诚访谈）
> "It wasn't an issue of vision. It was an issue of execution... I wasn't, at that time, capable, I don't think, of running the company as a whole. I was 30 years old. And I don't think I had enough experience to run a $2 billion company. Unfortunately, John didn't either."
>
> "Microsoft has no taste. They have absolutely no taste... They don't think of original ideas, and they don't bring much culture into their products."
>
> "We have always been shameless about stealing great ideas."

**来源**：allaboutstevejobs.com 完整 transcript | https://allaboutstevejobs.com/videos/misc/triumph_of_the_nerds_interview_1995  
**证据等级**：【一级·一手原话】  
**说话者**：Steve Jobs

### 4. 2007 D5 大会与比尔·盖茨同台访谈
> "Bill built the first software company in the industry... that was huge... Bill was really focused on software before almost anybody else had a clue."
>
> "We've kept our marriage secret for over a decade."

**来源**：allthingsd.com 完整 transcript | https://allthingsd.com/20070531/d5-gates-jobs-transcript  
**证据等级**：【一级·一手原话】  
**说话者**：Steve Jobs

### 5. 1997 WWDC 回归演讲
> "People think focus means saying yes to the thing you've got to focus on. But that's not what it means at all. It means saying no to the hundred other good ideas that there are. You have to pick carefully."

**来源**：xueqiu 引述 | https://xueqiu.com/3529462012/384994718  
**证据等级**：【二级·一手引语二手记录】  
**说话者**：Steve Jobs

## 核心引语验证（常见误归因核验）

| 引语 | 真实出处 | 验证结果 |
|------|----------|----------|
| "Stay Hungry, Stay Foolish" | 《Whole Earth Catalog》1968 停刊语，Jobs 引用 | Jobs 引用而非原创，验证通过 |
| "People don't know what they want until you show it to them" | Jobs 本人原话（多个访谈印证） | 真实一手，验证通过 |
| "Design is not just what it looks like and feels like. Design is how it works" | Jobs 本人原话 | 真实一手，验证通过 |
| "Good artists copy, great artists steal" | 源于 Pablo Picasso，Jobs 引用 | Jobs 引用而非原创，验证通过 |
| "Innovation distinguishes between a leader and a follower" | 广泛归因于 Jobs，可查证二手来源 | 【二级·二手归因待核验】 |
